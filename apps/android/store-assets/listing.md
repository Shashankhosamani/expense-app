## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Costiq turns your bank's SMS alerts into a running expense tracker — no manual entry, no spreadsheets.

HOW IT WORKS
Every time your bank texts you about a payment, Costiq reads it, pulls out the amount, merchant, and payment method, and adds it to your expense log. Salary credits, refunds, and other bank messages are sorted automatically — you can also mark specific credits (like a friend paying you back) so they offset your spending instead of skewing it.

WHAT YOU GET
• Automatic expense capture from bank SMS — works across banks and UPI apps
• A monthly overview: total spent, category breakdown, top merchants
• Budgets with month-over-month comparisons
• A review queue for anything Costiq isn't fully sure about
• Manual expense entry for cash or anything SMS misses
• Full edit history on every transaction

YOUR MESSAGES, HANDLED CAREFULLY
Costiq is built around one rule: read only what's needed, upload only what matters.
• Every message is sorted on your phone, before anything is sent anywhere
• One-time passwords and verification codes are dropped immediately and never uploaded
• Messages from personal contacts are ignored — only bank/payment senders are read
• Anything uploaded is encrypted before it's stored, and only decrypted to read the transaction amount
• Turn off SMS access anytime — your existing expense history stays, new messages simply stop being read

Costiq is for anyone who wants to see where their money goes without doing the bookkeeping themselves.
