SMS access, in detail

Costiq requests the READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS permissions for one purpose: turning your bank's transaction alerts into expense entries automatically, without you typing anything in.

Every incoming message is run through an on-device filter before anything happens with it:

  • Sender shape check — only messages from short-code, bank-style senders (e.g. HDFCBK) are considered at all. Messages from ordinary phone numbers or contacts are ignored.
  • Hard exclusions — anything that looks like a one-time password, verification code, or account/KYC notice is dropped immediately and never leaves your device.
  • Transaction signal check — what's left is only uploaded if it contains both a currency amount and a transaction verb ("debited", "credited", "spent", etc.); ambiguous messages are queued for a lower-confidence review, and everything else is discarded.

You can decline SMS access entirely and add every expense by hand instead, or turn it off later from Settings — expenses already recorded are kept; new messages simply stop being read.

How we use it

  • To build your expense list, monthly summaries, budgets, and spending insights.
  • To let you review, correct, or delete any transaction.
  • To operate account sign-in and keep your data scoped to your account only.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Storage & security

Transaction data and uploaded message text are stored on Supabase infrastructure. Message text is encrypted at rest with a key derived per-account — it is only decrypted server-side to extract the transaction amount and merchant, and is never displayed back to you as raw message text in the app.

Data in transit is sent over HTTPS.

Sharing

We don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Your data is processed by:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and storage infrastructure.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google, to verify your identity.

We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. Deleting a transaction removes it from your active list; you can request full account deletion at any time by contacting us below, which removes your account, transactions, and any stored message content.

Your choices

  • Decline or revoke SMS permission at any time from your device's app settings.
  • Edit or delete any transaction from within the app.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us below.

Children's privacy

Costiq is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
